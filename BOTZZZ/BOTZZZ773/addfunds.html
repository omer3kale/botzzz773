Frequently Asked Questions

Which gateways do you support?

Cryptomus handles all live crypto deposits while Heleket returns shortly after maintenance. Both gateways offer fast, automated balance crediting.

How long does it take to add funds?

Funds are typically added to your account instantly after successful payment. In rare cases, it may take up to 5 minutes for the balance to reflect.

What is the minimum deposit amount?

The minimum deposit amount is $1.00. You can add any amount above this minimum using preset amounts or entering a custom value.

Are there any fees?

A small processing fee of 1.00 % is applied to all transactions to cover payment gateway costs. The total amount including fees is shown before payment.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! Crypto checkouts redirect you to Heleket or Cryptomus secure pages and we never store your wallet credentials.

Can I get a refund?

Due to the instant nature of our services, added funds are non-refundable. Please ensure you're adding the correct amount before proceeding.
